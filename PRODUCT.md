# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

SMEs and midsize companies equally, evaluating custom software, integrations
and process improvements. The primary industry and buyer role remain open
decisions.

## Product Purpose

NOVAIX's public website explains its services and helps prospective customers
explore relevant solutions and arrange an initial conversation.

## Positioning

Confirmed by the user: custom software and integrations first, with AI when
it adds value. NOVAIX should not be presented solely as an AI assistant service.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The existing site describes custom software, digitization, integrations,
automation and applied AI. Custom projects are scoped individually; packaged
automation plans are a separate offer. Spanish and English entry points,
sector pages, video demonstrations, team information and public legal pages
already exist. The implementation is a static website.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve the NOVAIX name and existing company logo. The identity-preserving
dark homepage has been implemented and published; this is not a redesign mandate
for sector landings.
Preserve the existing dark navy/black palette and cyan accents. The user explicitly
rejected the light/white-background concept; explore composition within the dark
identity rather than replacing the brand palette.
The existing planet and blue/orange NOVAIX logo are core company identity and
must remain visually prominent, including in mobile proposals. Do not replace
them with a generic wordmark or reduce them to incidental decorative details.

## Evidence on Hand

Current website content and assets are in this repository. The source of numerical case-study
claims has not been validated during the design review; do not invent evidence,
customer endorsements or measured conversion improvements. Label synthetic
demonstrations clearly.

## Product Principles

- Explain the business problem before the technology.
- Distinguish custom projects from packaged automation services.
- Preserve a clear path from exploration to an initial conversation.
- Keep illustrative workflows separate from verified customer outcomes.

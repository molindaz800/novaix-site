Frequently asked questions

What NOVAIX automates and where people remain in control

Does the AI decide routes or prices?

No. It collects and structures information; operational and pricing decisions remain with the team.

Can it provide shipment updates?

Yes, when connected to an authorised source with precisely defined status updates it is allowed to share.

Is it suitable for small transport companies?

Yes. The system adapts to your actual workload and can start with one workflow, such as quotes or issue management.

From today’s process to a solution in use

The scope might be a straightforward integration or custom software. The method helps us decide what is worth building.

  1. 01

    Understand

    Review how the work happens today, who is involved and which tools are used.

    A shared description of the process and its starting point.

  2. 02

    Prioritise

    Choose a manageable improvement and agree limits, dependencies and what to observe.

    Scope, deliverables and acceptance criteria.

  3. 03

    Implement

    Configure, integrate or build. Test the workflow with the people who will use it.

    A tested solution, rollout and guidance for use.

  4. 04

    Check and adjust

    Review usage, issues and the agreed indicators. Propose changes where they add value.

    A review of how it works and agreed next steps.

The first conversation is about fit. If a technical study with its own work and deliverables is needed, we explain and quote it before starting.

Security and data protection

Digital transformation should not mean losing control. Our approach puts privacy, traceability and data minimization at the center of every solution.

Control of information is part of the project

Before development, we define what data the solution needs, what it is used for, who can see it, where it is hosted and how it is retained. The aim is to build a useful, measurable system proportionate to the process it improves.

  • Data map and processing purpose before activating customer workflows.
  • Configuration aligned with GDPR and LOPD-GDD: minimization, limited retention and user rights.
  • Providers, integrations and hosting reviewed case by case; EU infrastructure available.
  • Human escalation for sensitive cases, incidents or requests AI should not handle alone.

Controls we define with you

Encryption and secure channels HTTPS connections and encryption in transit in integrated services that support it.
Least-privilege access Separate roles, permissions and credentials to limit who can read or modify data.
Operational traceability Key event logging to review processes, changes, integrations and automations.
Only necessary data We process only the information each solution needs to fulfil its purpose.
Processors and providers We document external tools such as Calendly, CRM, n8n or messaging channels.
AI guardrails Response limits, escalation and human review to prevent use outside the agreed scope.

Each project is adapted to the real data processing involved: we do not promise a one-size-fits-all setup, we design the right security level for your business and sector.

Before we start

Do I need to know which solution I want?

No. Tell us what happens today, where time is lost or where follow-up breaks down. We can also start from a tool or a technical requirement you already have.

Do we have to replace our CRM or ERP?

Not necessarily. First we check what can be done with your existing tools and the access or integrations they allow. Any limitations are explained before scope is committed.

Will you use AI for everything?

No. A rule, integration or existing tool may be a better fit. We use AI when it has a useful role and its work can be reviewed.

Does the first conversation commit us to a paid study?

No. The initial conversation is free and carries no commitment. It helps us understand the context and consider a next step; it is not a full audit or a demo connected to your systems.

What happens after delivery?

We agree the rollout and initial follow-up. If maintenance or further support is needed, coverage, capacity and terms are defined separately.

How are data and decisions protected?

We review necessary data, access, providers and controls for each project. We define what the solution can do and when a person must step in. Do not send credentials or sensitive data in your initial enquiry.
